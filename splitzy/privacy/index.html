Privacy Policy for Splitzy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Splitzy ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Francisco Jesús Sánchez Muñoz, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user data.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: If you sign in using Google or Apple, we store your email address and display name solely to identify your account and associate you with shared expense groups.
  • Guest Access: If you join as a guest without creating an account, we use Firebase Anonymous Authentication. No email or personal identifiers are collected.
  • Group Expense Data: Group names, member display names, expense descriptions, dates, amounts, and settlement records are stored in Google Cloud Firestore to enable real-time collaboration.

2. Advertising & Analytics

  • Google AdMob: We monetize Splitzy exclusively through Google AdMob advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS or Advertising ID on Android) and IP addresses to serve ads.
  • Consent Management (GDPR / UMP & Apple ATT): For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK, we present Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before serving ads. On iOS, we request tracking authorization via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. You can change your ad preferences at any time in the app Settings.
  • Crashlytics & Analytics: We use Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to detect application errors and measure performance anonymously.

3. Data Security & Storage

All data transmitted between the Splitzy application and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS encryption. Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure adhering to rigorous security standards.

4. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have the right to request deletion of your account and personal data at any time. You can delete your account directly within the app Settings or by emailing our support team.
