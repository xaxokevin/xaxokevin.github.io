Privacy Policy for PennyPlan

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

PennyPlan ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by Francisco Jesús Sánchez Muñoz, is committed to safeguarding your financial privacy. This policy explains how your financial data is handled.

1. Data We Collect & Store

  • Local SQLite Storage: All your transactions, expense categories, and monthly payroll budget plans are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database.
  • Couple Synchronization: If you use Couple Mode, shared ledger records are encrypted and synced via Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Authentication: Optional Google or Apple Sign-In stores your email only to associate your paired partner accounts.

2. Advertising & Analytics

  • Google AdMob: Monetized exclusively via Google AdMob native ads. Device advertising identifiers may be used in compliance with user consent.
  • Google UMP & Apple ATT: In the EEA/UK, consent is evaluated via Google UMP prior to ad initialization. On iOS, App Tracking Transparency is requested following UMP.
  • Diagnostics: Crashlytics diagnostic data is collected anonymously to fix app errors.

3. Data Deletion & Export

You can export your complete transaction history to JSON or delete your cloud account and all synced couple data at any time from app Settings.
