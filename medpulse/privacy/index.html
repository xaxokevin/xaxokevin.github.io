Privacy Policy for MedPulse

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Zero Cloud Health Data: MedPulse stores 100% of your medication schedules, dosages, and intake histories locally and encrypted on your device. We do not transmit or store medical records on any remote cloud server.

1. Data Collection & Medical Disclaimers

MedPulse is strictly an informational medication reminder tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, prescription advice, or clinical guidance. Always consult licensed healthcare professionals.

2. Advertising & Restful Experience

  • Silent Native Ads: We monetize MedPulse with Google AdMob native ads. To protect resting patients, all audio and autoplay video is strictly disabled.
  • Consent Management: We enforce Google UMP (GDPR) and Apple ATT compliance. Consent can be revoked at any time in Settings.

3. Diagnostic Analytics

Crashlytics reports non-identifiable crash dumps to maintain high reliability across device reboots and low-power modes.
